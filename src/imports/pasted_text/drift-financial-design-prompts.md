Drift Financial — Page-by-Page Design Prompts
Adapted from a bold corporate FinTech landing page style into the Drift Financial brand system. Every prompt below is self-contained — hand it to a designer or AI builder and it should produce a complete page.

Global Design Rules (Apply to ALL Pages)
BRAND COLORS
  Primary:   Drift Navy #00263A  |  Drift Gold #B8975B  |  Drift Silver #A7A8A9  |  Crisp White #FFFFFF
  Secondary: Light Gold #F8E59A  |  Soft Blue #8BD3E6   |  Deep Navy #1D1F2A
TYPOGRAPHY
  Headings (H1–H6): Montserrat Bold 700
  Body / Buttons:   Inter Medium 500
  Button labels:    Inter Medium 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em
GLOBAL PATTERNS
  - Rounded corners: 12px buttons, 16px cards
  - Pill-shaped CTAs: Drift Gold bg + Deep Navy text
  - Section striping: alternate Drift Navy / White / Light Gold backgrounds
  - Hover lift on cards: translateY(-4px), box-shadow
  - 300ms ease transitions, smooth scroll
  - 80px vertical padding desktop, 48px mobile
